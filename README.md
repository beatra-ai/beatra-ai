# Beatra

Beatra is an AI media generation platform; its Agent Skills run inside Claude Code, Codex, OpenClaw and other SKILL.md agents and render with Beatra credits.

## Images

- [ai-media-generator-skill](https://github.com/beatra-ai/ai-media-generator-skill) — Create AI images, videos, music, and voice-over from one Agent Skill for Claude Code, Codex, and OpenClaw.
- [ai-logo-maker-skill](https://github.com/beatra-ai/ai-logo-maker-skill) — Turn a brand name, industry, or reference image into logo concepts and a scalable brand mark, from inside Claude Code, Codex, or OpenClaw.
- [ai-poster-maker-skill](https://github.com/beatra-ai/ai-poster-maker-skill) — Turn an event description, a product photo, or brand references into an event poster, promo banner, or social media graphic with clear visual hierarchy, category-matched style, and space kept for text, from inside Claude Code, Codex, or OpenClaw.
- [ai-image-generator-skill](https://github.com/beatra-ai/ai-image-generator-skill) — Generate, compose, and edit product images, brand visuals, posters, social graphics, illustrations, and photo variations, from inside Claude Code, Codex, or OpenClaw.
- [photo-to-anime-skill](https://github.com/beatra-ai/photo-to-anime-skill) — Turn a photo into anime, manga, comic, cartoon, watercolor, clay, or 3D character art while the subject stays recognizable, and reuse the same look across a whole set, from inside Claude Code, Codex, or OpenClaw.
- [ai-headshot-generator-skill](https://github.com/beatra-ai/ai-headshot-generator-skill) — Turn a casual selfie into a professional headshot with a new background, business attire, and studio lighting for resumes, company websites, career profiles, and social media, from inside Claude Code, Codex, or OpenClaw.
- [ai-image-realism-skill](https://github.com/beatra-ai/ai-image-realism-skill) — Make existing AI portraits, product images, and marketing visuals look more natural by fixing plastic skin, malformed hands and faces, repeated textures, and inconsistent lighting, from inside Claude Code, Codex, or OpenClaw.
- [ai-storyboard-generator-skill](https://github.com/beatra-ai/ai-storyboard-generator-skill) — Turn a script, scene, or ad brief into a practical shot list and one to four storyboard key-frame images, from inside Claude Code, Codex, or OpenClaw.

## Video

- [talking-avatar-video-skill](https://github.com/beatra-ai/talking-avatar-video-skill) — Turn one portrait and a short script into a talking avatar video, from inside Claude Code, Codex, or OpenClaw.
- [ai-video-generator-skill](https://github.com/beatra-ai/ai-video-generator-skill) — Generate, animate, edit, and extend short AI videos for ads, product stories, social posts, b-roll, transitions, and cinematic concepts, from inside Claude Code, Codex, or OpenClaw.
- [viral-video-remake-skill](https://github.com/beatra-ai/viral-video-remake-skill) — Break a short video that worked into its hook, beats, and call to action, then rebuild that structure around your own subject as a shot list, beat frames, and narration, delivered as one vertical clip or as segmented sources with captions and a timecoded edit list, from inside Claude Code, Codex, or OpenClaw.
